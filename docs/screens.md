# Screens

Every screen in SignuDoc, on a computer and on a phone. The organization, people and documents are made up.

## Sending

### Documents

Where every document stands, what needs you, and the documents grouped by when they were made.

![Documents](images/screens/dashboard.png)

### New envelope

Drop a PDF or a Word file, or write the document here.

![New envelope](images/screens/new.png)

### Writing a document

An editor that works like a word processor, with starting points and Word, web page, Markdown and plain-text import.

![Document composer](images/screens/composer.png)

### Who signs

Signers in the order they sign, people who get a copy, and how each signer is checked.

![Who signs](images/screens/signers.png)

### Placing fields

Signature, initials, date, name, text, number, checkbox, dropdown and radio fields, each tied to one signer.

![Placing fields](images/screens/prepare.png)

### Review and send

The deadline, automatic reminders and what happens once it is sent.

![Review and send](images/screens/review.png)

### A sent envelope

Who it waits on, the signers, the deadline and reminders.

![A sent envelope](images/screens/envelope.png)

### A completed envelope

The signed PDF, the audit certificate and everyone who signed.

![A completed envelope](images/screens/completed.png)

## Signing

### Signing a document

Signers need no account. Fields from earlier signers stay visible and locked.

![Signing](images/screens/sign.png)

### In your own colors

An install's own logo and brand color on the signing pages and in every email.

![Signing with an organization's branding](images/screens/branding.png)

### Verify

Anyone can check that a signed copy is exactly the file the site produced.

![Verify](images/screens/verify.png)
